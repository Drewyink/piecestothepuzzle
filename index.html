Overview · The Pieces to the Puzzle Story

Every entrepreneur begins with a vision.

A vision to build something impactful and purposeful. To serve others through their expertise. To create greater freedom, flexibility, and ownership over their future.

The Turning Point

Yet somewhere along the journey, many business owners find themselves consumed by the very businesses they worked so hard to build. Instead of focusing on their purpose, they become overwhelmed by administrative tasks, operational bottlenecks, inconsistent systems, and the endless demands of running a growing business.

What began as a pursuit of freedom quickly becomes a source of stress. Pieces to the Puzzle was created to change that.

Why We Exist

Developed from a passion for helping people, organizing complexity, and creating structure where chaos exists, the company was built on one belief: successful businesses should not feel overwhelming to run.

Entrepreneurs possess extraordinary talent and vision, but often lack the operational systems necessary to support sustainable growth. The absence of structure behind the scenes creates friction, burnout, and limits on future growth.

How We Help

Through strategic systems, thoughtful automation, operational support, and brand guidance, we help entrepreneurs transform fragmented processes into organized, efficient, and scalable foundations.

More than a consultant, Pieces to the Puzzle serves as a trusted action partner, helping clients build businesses that perform better and feel great to operate.

At its heart, Pieces to the Puzzle is about restoring what entrepreneurship was meant to provide: clarity, confidence, freedom, and the space to focus on what matters most.

Talent Acquisition & Recruiting

The most important piece of any business is its people. We help you find them.

Pieces to the Puzzle provides professional recruiting services for companies of every size and industry. We manage the full talent acquisition lifecycle, from role definition through signed offer, delivering vetted, qualified candidates through a structured, confidential process.

1

Needs Assessment & Role Definition

Every engagement begins with a structured consultation with your leadership team. We develop a comprehensive position profile covering core responsibilities, required competencies, performance expectations for the first year, team dynamics, and organizational culture. We also provide market-aligned compensation guidance to ensure your offer is competitive before the search begins.

2

Talent Sourcing & Market Outreach

We execute a multi-channel sourcing strategy that combines professional networks, industry-specific channels, referral pipelines, and targeted direct outreach. This approach reaches both active job seekers and high-performing passive candidates who are not visible on job boards, giving your organization access to the full talent market rather than a fraction of it.

3

Screening & Qualification

Each candidate undergoes a rigorous, consistent evaluation: detailed resume and credential review, structured competency-based interviews, skills verification, and an assessment of alignment with your culture and compensation range. You receive a curated shortlist of pre-vetted, genuinely interested candidates, accompanied by written evaluation summaries for each.

4

Interview Coordination & Offer Management

We manage the interview process end to end: scheduling, candidate preparation, structured feedback collection after each round, and timely communication that protects your employer brand. When you are ready to extend an offer, we advise on offer structure, manage the negotiation professionally, and guide both parties through to a signed acceptance.

5

Onboarding & Retention Support

Our responsibility does not end at the signed offer. We support pre-boarding communication, onboarding workflow design, and documentation, then conduct structured 30, 60, and 90-day check-ins with both the new hire and the hiring manager. Backed by our operations practice, this ensures every placement is positioned to succeed and stay.

  • Contract, interim & part-time staffing
  • Executive & specialized searches
